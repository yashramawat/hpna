About HPNAI

A professional home for hospice & palliative care nurses

HPNAI is a professional nursing association dedicated to the promotion and welfare of hospice and palliative care nurses. The association works to empower nurses through education, recognition, leadership, advocacy, mentorship, and research. It promotes a multidisciplinary approach to provide affordable, accessible, and appropriate quality care to patients facing life-limiting or prolonged disabling illness and their caregivers.

Who We Are

A not-for-profit association for palliative care nurses

The Hospice and Palliative Care Nurses Association India (HPNAI) is a not-for-profit professional association formed for the promotion and welfare of nurses involved or interested in hospice and palliative care. The association aims to create a platform for nursing professionals, nursing educators, students, institutions, and healthcare workers to collaborate for the advancement of palliative care nursing in India.

HPNAI focuses on education, advocacy, leadership, mentorship, research, and multidisciplinary collaboration. The association supports nurses in improving their professional competence and strengthening their role within the healthcare system.

Mission

To advance palliative care and hospice nursing in India through dedication and commitment to exemplary nursing, competence, advocacy, leadership, mentorship, and research.

Vision

HPNAI envisions a healthcare system where all patients facing life-limiting or prolonged disabling illness receive affordable, accessible, appropriate, and quality care through a multidisciplinary approach — while supporting caregivers and promoting ethical care for terminally ill patients.

Legal Status

Registration & Legal Status

The Hospice and Palliative Care Nurses Association India (HPNAI) is a registered professional association under the Rajasthan Societies Registration Act, 1958. The association is registered at District Jodhpur with Registration No. COOP/2022/JODHPUR/203395, dated 13-10-2022.
